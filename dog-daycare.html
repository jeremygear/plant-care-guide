Why Choose Dog Daycare?

Dog daycare can help pets stay active and entertained while their owners are away. Regular exercise, supervised interaction, and structured routines can support a dog's physical and mental well-being.

At Hawkeye Paws, our fictional care team organizes activities based on each dog's personality, comfort level, and energy.

A Typical Day at Hawkeye Paws

Morning Welcome

Dogs arrive and are welcomed by our care team. Staff review any special instructions and help each dog settle in.

Supervised Playtime

Dogs enjoy supervised play sessions and activities suited to their size and temperament.

Afternoon Rest

Quiet rest periods give dogs an opportunity to relax between activities.

Evening Pickup

Owners pick up their dogs after a day of exercise, enrichment, and attention.
