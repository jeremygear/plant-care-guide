Affordable Dog Boarding in Iowa City

Finding dependable dog boarding shouldn't mean spending a fortune. At Hawkeye Paws, we offer affordable overnight stays with personalized attention, comfortable sleeping spaces, and supervised playtime.

Our standard boarding package starts at just $35 per night, making it a convenient option for Iowa City dog owners, college students, and families.

Our Affordable Boarding Packages

Standard Stay

Includes a comfortable sleeping space, scheduled feeding, fresh water, outdoor breaks, and daily supervised playtime.

Extended Stay

What's Included in Every Stay?

Every dog at Hawkeye Paws receives attentive care regardless of the package selected. Our affordable dog boarding services include clean sleeping areas, fresh drinking water, scheduled meals, regular bathroom breaks, and supervised exercise.

Our fictional staff members follow each dog's feeding instructions and work to create a calm, welcoming environment. Owners are encouraged to bring their dog's regular food and favorite comfort item.

Why Affordable Doesn't Mean Lower Quality

We believe responsible pet care should be accessible to more families. Instead of charging extra for essential daily care, Hawkeye Paws includes basic services in every boarding package.

Our fictional founder, Emma Collins, designed the standard boarding program to balance comfort, safety, and reasonable pricing for the Iowa City community.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does dog boarding cost?

At Hawkeye Paws, standard dog boarding starts at $35 per night. Extended stays of seven nights or longer cost $32 per night in our fictional pricing model.

Can I board two dogs together?

Yes. Our two-dog family package is designed for owners who want to board two dogs during the same stay.

Do I need to bring dog food?

We recommend bringing your dog's usual food to help maintain a consistent diet during their stay.
